# Google Vids: step by step

Everything you need is in `submission/vids-clips/`: **15 files numbered in order**, 4 title cards (PNG) and 11 screen clips (MP4). Each file is one scene. Each scene gets one voiceover from the table below.

## A. Get the files into Google Drive (2 min)
1. Open **drive.google.com** → **New → Folder upload** → choose the `vids-clips` folder.
   (Path on this laptop: `/home/charizard/fast/submission/vids-clips`)

## B. Create the video (1 min)
2. Open **vids.google.com** → **Blank video** (not "Help me create"; we already have every scene).
3. Click the title at top left → rename it to **HawkPath demo**.
4. Optional, under the gear or **Video settings**: keep **16:9**.

## C. Build the 15 scenes (8–10 min)
For each row in the table, in order:
5. In the scene list on the left (or the timeline at the bottom), click **+ / New scene → Blank**.
6. **Insert → Drive & Photos** (or the **Uploads** panel), pick the file from `vids-clips`, and stretch it to fill the whole frame.
   - **PNG cards:** set the scene length to about **8 seconds**.
   - **MP4 clips:** they already have the right length. Leave them.
7. Delete the empty first scene Vids creates, if there is one.

## D. Add the voiceover to each scene (10 min)
8. Select a scene → open **Voiceover** (or **Scripts**) in the right sidebar → choose **Current scene**.
9. Paste that scene's text from the table → **Generate voiceover** → pick **one calm voice** and use the same voice for every scene → **Insert**.
10. Make sure **"Extend scenes to match voiceovers"** is ON. Short clips then hold their last frame while the sentence finishes.
11. Repeat for all 15 scenes.
    - Prefer your own voice? Use **Record → Voiceover** on each scene and read the same text.

## E. Polish (5 min, optional)
12. **Music:** right sidebar **Music** → pick a calm, minimal track → set the volume low (about 10–15%).
13. **Transitions:** keep them simple (Fade or None) between scenes.
14. **Captions:** if Vids offers **Captions → On**, turn them on. Judges often watch muted.

## F. Check and export (5 min)
15. Click **Play** and watch it end to end. The total should be about **2:20–2:40** (hard limit 3:00).
    - Too long? Trim the end of `10-compare-schools` or `12-transcript-import`: hover over the clip edge and drag inward.
16. **File → Download → MP4** (or **Export to Drive**).
17. **youtube.com → Create → Upload video** → title: *HawkPath: Voice what-if transfer planning (CRC Hacks 2026)* → visibility **Unlisted** or **Public**. Not "Private", or judges can't watch it.
18. Copy the YouTube link into Devpost.

## Notes
- If a menu name differs slightly, Google moves things around. Voiceover lives in the right-side panel, and uploads live under **Insert**.
- AI voiceover is English only, with up to 2,500 characters per script. Every block above is well under that.
- The voiceover already includes the required disclosures (demo students and the counselor preview use example data).
